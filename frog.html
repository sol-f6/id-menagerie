A. callidryas,

red-eyed tree frog

a colorful native to the central and south american rainforest. females tend to grow larger than males. its diet mainly consists of insects and occasionally small amphibians. red-eyed tree frog eggs sense external stimuli before hatching to ascertain the best chance of survival. the red-eyed tree frog tends to be slimy and untrustworthy, but makes a wonderful friend nonetheless. it is great with money. it jumps from group to group, able to strike up conversation with anyone but leaving with few true connections. it hates the beach.

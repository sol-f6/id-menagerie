a large lagomorph with a white tail and muscular hind legs. largely herbivorous. it is normally shy and keeps to the nighttime, but becomes excitable in the daylight of springtime. battles fellow hares to impress mates or ward off others. constantly aware of being double-crossed. fiercely loyal to friends and unforgiving of ene-mies. charismatic and tends to get its way. always wary of snakes and tigers. will always get along with sheep. may not get along with other hares.
